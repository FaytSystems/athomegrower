Plant care

Light:

Full sun to part sun

Soil mix:

Adaptable, well-drained soil

Soil moisture:

Moderate moisture during establishment; mature trees tolerate some dryness.

When to water:

Water deeply while establishing

Fertilizer:

Use light fertility only if growth is weak; excess nitrogen can increase fruit drop.

Pruning:

Minimal pruning beyond removing dead, broken, crossing, or crowded limbs.

Repotting/rootbound:

For patio trees, use large containers, refresh soil, root-prune when necessary, and avoid letting the root ball dry out.

Best use

Native edible landscapes and wildlife gardens

Zones: usda zones 4-9

Problems to watch

Watch for scale, persimmon psyllid, borers, leafrollers, fruit drop, and leaf spots.
